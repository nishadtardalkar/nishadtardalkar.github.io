False Trail Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

False Trail (“the App”) is a choose-your-own-adventure game published by OkayishGames. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

The App may store gameplay progress locally on your device (for example, using on-device preferences) so you can continue your story. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-party services

The App does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social login. Distribution through Google Play or the Apple App Store is subject to those platforms’ own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
