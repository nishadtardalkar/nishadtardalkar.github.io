Privacy Policy for Unfair Flips

Last updated: February 3, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Unfair Flips ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

Unfair Flips is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The app may store the following data locally on your device only (not sent to any server):

  • Game progress: in-game balance, win streak, upgrade levels, and similar gameplay data so you can continue where you left off.
  • App preferences: sound on/off setting and selected theme (e.g., classic, Halloween, Christmas, space).
  • First-launch flag: a simple indicator that you have opened the app before (used only to show an optional first-time experience).

This data stays on your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

2. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions on Android:

  • RECORD_AUDIO / MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used only to play in-app sound effects (e.g., coin flip, coin drop). We do not record, store, or transmit any audio from your device.

3. Data Use

Any data stored locally is used solely to:

  • Restore your game progress and settings when you reopen the app.
  • Provide the features you choose (sounds, theme).

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

4. Third Parties

We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or social login. Your local data is not shared with third parties. Google Play may collect its own data (e.g., installs, crashes) as described in Google's policies; that is governed by Google, not this app.

5. Data Security

Data is stored only on your device using the device's standard secure storage. We do not transmit or store your data on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Unfair Flips does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require registration or submission of personal data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

7. Data Deletion

To delete all app data, you can uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Unfair Flips → Storage → Clear data). We do not retain copies of your data on our systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we may notify you via the app or the Play Store listing.
